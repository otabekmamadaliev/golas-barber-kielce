A barber shop for men who know what they want

Gola's Barber Shop is at Henryka Sienkiewicza 76 — on the main promenade in Kielce, in the arcade locals call "pod filarami". It is the kind of place you drop into on your way across town and leave with a haircut done properly.

We work calmly and carefully. Every visit starts with a short conversation about how you want to look and how much time you realistically spend on your hair each morning — the scissors come out after that.

A 4.9 rating from 192 Google reviews is the best evidence we have that the approach works.

Services

The full range of men's barbering — from a quick tidy-up to a complete change of hair and beard.

  1. 1 Haircut Classic and modern cuts, matched to your head shape and hair type. Finished with clippers and scissors. 80–120 zł
  2. 2 Straight-razor shave A traditional hot-towel shave — lather, oil and a soothing balm afterwards. 100 zł
  3. 3 Beard shaping Line-up, trimming to length, moustache styling and a razor finish. 80–110 zł
  4. 4 Hair + beard Both in one visit. The most popular choice — hair and beard shaped to suit each other. 130–180 zł
  5. 5 Ear and nose trimming Quick, safe removal of stray hair from ears, nose and neckline. Usually added to a haircut. prices in the shop
  6. 6 Advice and grooming Help choosing a cut, and the products to keep it looking right at home. included in the visit
  7. 7 Colouring Covering grey and evening out hair or beard colour. 80 zł

Prices come from the shop's own Booksy profile. The ranges are there because Booksy lists several rates for some services, depending on the barber. The exact price is confirmed when you book.
